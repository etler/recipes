# Creamy Miso Butter Sauce

* 2 tbsp white miso
* 2 tbsp butter
* 1/2 shallot, chopped
* 1 cup half and half or heavy cream

1. Char white miso on a pan or under a broiler until brown and dry. Do not burn.
1. Scrape up miso and mix with butter in a pan, add shallots and saute.
1. Slowly mix in half and half, stirring until smooth and combined.
1. Reduce until sauce reaches desired consistency.
