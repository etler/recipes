# Greek Yogurt Pancakes

* 1 cup rolled oats, blended
* 1 cup greek yogurt
* 2 eggs, separated
* 1 tbsp maple syrup to taste
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 2 tsp baking powder
* 1/4 tsp cinnamon
* Pinch of salt
* 1-3 tbsp milk as needed

1. Blend rolled oats until fine
2. Whip egg whites until fluffy
3. Combine yogurt, yolks, maple syrup, and vanilla
4. Mix in oats, baking power, cinnamon, and salt. Fold in egg whites, add milk until batter has thick but runny consistency.
6. Cook 1/4 cup of batter per pancake on skillet on medium low heat, flipping when bubbles pop.
