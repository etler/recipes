# Tamago Sando (Japanese Egg Sandwich)

* 3 large eggs, hard boiled, peeled
* 1/4 tsp sugar
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 1/8 tsp black pepper
* 2 tsp milk
* 2 tbsp Kewpie mayonnaise
* 4 slices shokupan milk bread
* butter

1. Mash eggs in bowl with a fork
1. Mix in ingredients
1. Butter bread, spread filling, stack and weigh down
1. Cut off crust, cut in half
1. Refrigerate in airtight container up to 2 days
