# Squash and Brie Stuffed Shells

* 1/2 kabocha squash
* 4-6oz brie
* 1-2 tbsp butter
* sage, chopped
* 1/2 shallot, chopped
* 1 egg
* lemon zest
* salt
* pepper
* ~20 large Conchiglioni shells
* mozzarella or parmesan cheese
* [1 cup creamy miso butter sauce](/creamy-miso-butter-sauce.md)

1. Steam squash 15-20 min until soft and remove skin.
1. Mix together squash and brie, allow to cool.
1. Cook Shells until al-dente, allow to cool.
1. Preheat oven to 350°F
1. Grease large casserole dish with butter or with bechamel bottom
1. Add egg and lemon zest to cooled mixture. Add salt and pepper to taste. Season lightly, miso sauce will introduce salt.
1. Fill shells with cooled filling and arrange in a casserole dish filling side up, pour over half of the miso sauce then top with cheese.
1. Bake ~20 min covered, then ~10 min until cheese is melted and browned.
1. Top with rest of creamy miso sauce and serve.
