# Ceasar Salad Dressing

* 3 cloves garlic
* salt
* 1 1/2 anchovy filets in oil, drained
* 1 egg yolks
* 1 lemon, zested, juiced
* 1/2 tbsp Dijon mustard
* 1/4 cup vegetable oil
* 1/4 cup greek yogurt
* 1/2 cup Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated

1. Grate 1 garlic clove, finely mince the rest with salt, chopping and smearing.
1. Finely chop the anchovies until a paste forms. Chop and smear to combine the anchovies and garlic together. Transfer this mixture to a medium bowl.
1. Add egg yolk, lemon juice, and mustard. Season to taste with salt and whisk together until evenly combined.
1. Slowly add vegetable oil while whisking, a few drops at a time, until the dressing starts to become creamy and emulsified. Slowly increase pouring speed to a steady stream.
1. Stir in the grated Parmigiano Reggiano.
